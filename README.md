# 🌾 Fazenda Ativa

**Mapas profissionais, informações organizadas, gestão financeira e operacional e Inteligência Artificial — tudo em um aplicativo simples, feito para funcionar na realidade do campo, mesmo sem internet.**

Smart management app for rural properties — professional maps, organized information, financial and operational management, and AI, all in one simple app built to work in the reality of the field, even offline.

---

## 📖 Overview

Fazenda Ativa is a smart management app for rural properties. It brings together professional maps, financial and operational management, and AI — everything working **offline**, directly in the field, without depending on a signal.

The goal is simple: give farmers a single, easy-to-use tool that fits the reality of rural work.

Features:

- 🗺️ **Professional, personalized maps** — work offline, directly in the field, without depending on a signal.
- 📋 **Simple, intuitive management** — expenses, incidents, herd, cash flow, and other farm information.
- 📂 **Information always at hand** — organized and ready whenever you need it.
- 🤖 **AI that extracts data and generates useful insights** — automatically.

### 📷 OCR — Register a transaction by photo

Recording a transaction is as simple as **taking a photo**: point the camera at a receipt, invoice, or note, and the AI does the rest. The OCR pipeline reads the document and extracts the relevant data automatically — no manual typing in the field.

> _Take a photo, the AI does the rest._

---

## 🎯 Why Fazenda Ativa?

Rural properties face a common set of problems:

- **No reliable internet** in the field.
- **Information scattered** across notebooks, spreadsheets, and memory.
- **Tools built for offices**, not for the reality of farm work.

Fazenda Ativa was designed from the ground up to solve these problems — **offline-first**, simple, and made for the field.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Mobile:** Android with **Kotlin** and **Jetpack Compose**
- **Maps:** Offline map rendering
- **Data:** Local-first storage with sync
- **AI & OCR:** On-device and cloud-based inference for document and image analysis
